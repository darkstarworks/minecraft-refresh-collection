## About this project

This is an improved & optimised **derivative** of a data pack originally by **[lukidonu](https://modrinth.com/user/lukidonu)**, who has since **discontinued all of their projects** (*"Projects are discontinued #RIP"*). Across several updates it's been substantially reworked — bug fixes, new features and optimisation — going well beyond the original. I tried to reach the author without success (see the note at the bottom).

- ✅ Added a built-in **loot editor** — tune every chest, barrel, pot & vault to your server

## Customise the loot 🎁

Want different rewards? The collection comes with a simple **loot editor** — no commands, no JSON. Open a plain-text settings file, change how common each item is, how many drop, and how full each container rolls, then double-click **Build-Loot** to apply it. You can add or remove items too, and misspelled item names are caught before anything is saved. Grab it from the [GitHub collection](https://github.com/darkstarworks/minecraft-refresh-collection) and read **HOW TO CHANGE LOOT.txt**.

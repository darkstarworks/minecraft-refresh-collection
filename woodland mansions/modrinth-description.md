Woodland Mansions **reimagined** — larger, more varied, and actually worth the long trek out to find one. This data pack replaces the repetitive vanilla mansion layout with bigger, more interesting structures to explore and traverse.

> **Works great with [TrialChamberPro](https://modrinth.com/plugin/trialchamberpro):** these mansions include **trial spawners and trial vaults**, so you can pair the pack with TCP (+ its premium modules) to snapshot, protect, auto-reset and manage each one just like a vanilla Trial Chamber — turning it into renewable, multiplayer-friendly loot content.

This is an improved & optimised **derivative** of a data pack originally by **[lukidonu](https://modrinth.com/user/lukidonu)**, who has since **discontinued all of their projects** (*"Projects are discontinued #RIP"*). ## Customise the loot 🎁

Want different rewards? The collection comes with a simple **loot editor** — no commands, no JSON. Open a plain-text settings file, change how common each item is, how many drop, and how full each container rolls, then double-click **Build-Loot** to apply it. You can add or remove items too, and misspelled item names are caught before anything is saved. Grab it from the [GitHub collection](https://github.com/darkstarworks/minecraft-refresh-collection) and read **HOW TO CHANGE LOOT.txt**.

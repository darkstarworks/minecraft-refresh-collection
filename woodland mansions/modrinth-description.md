### Woodland Mansions **reimagined**
Larger, more varied, and actually worth the long trek out to find one. This data pack replaces the repetitive vanilla mansion layout with bigger, more interesting structures to explore and traverse.

<br>

> **Works great with [Better Trial Chambers](https://modrinth.com/plugin/bettertrialchambers):** the **trial spawners** and **trial vaults** can be paired with BTC (+ its premium modules) to snapshot, protect, auto-reset and manage loot for each chamber easily.

<br>

## What's been done

- Works on **Minecraft 26.2 and 26.3** from one download
- Fixed MANY features that were broken or not behaving as intended
- Repackaged for **Fabric, Forge, NeoForge & Quilt** (data pack + mod)
- Added a built-in **loot editor** to tune every chest, barrel, pot & vault to your server

## Customise the loot

Want different rewards? This and the other data packs in this collection comes with a simple **loot editor**. <br>
No commands, no JSON. Just open a plain-text settings file, change how common each item is, how many drop, and how full each container rolls, then double-click **Build-Loot** to apply it. 

You can add or remove items too, and misspelled item names are caught before anything is saved. Grab it from the [GitHub collection](https://github.com/darkstarworks/minecraft-refresh-collection) and read **HOW TO CHANGE LOOT.txt**.

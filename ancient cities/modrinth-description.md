> **An improved & optimised derivative of Lukidonu's original** — substantially reworked, fixed and extended, and kept current with the latest Minecraft.

New **Ancient Cities** to get lost in — bigger, more varied, and far more rewarding to explore than the vanilla deep-dark layout. This data pack overhauls Ancient City generation so every city comes out larger, with more custom buildings, bridges and structures instead of the same handful of vanilla rooms — all while the Warden still lurks in the dark below.

> **Pairs perfectly with Better Ancient Cities:** these cities still generate as regular `minecraft:ancient_city` structures, so a management plugin like Better Ancient Cities (AncientCityPro) can discover, snapshot, protect, auto-reset and manage each one — turning it into renewable, multiplayer-friendly content. Find it alongside my other plugins in the [ESMP collection](https://modrinth.com/organization/esmp).

## About this project

This is an improved & optimised **derivative** of a data pack originally by **[lukidonu](https://modrinth.com/user/lukidonu)**, who has since **discontinued all of their projects** (*"Projects are discontinued #RIP"*). Across several updates it's been substantially reworked — bug fixes, new features and optimisation — going well beyond the original. I tried to reach the author without success (see the note at the bottom).

## Customise the loot 🎁

Want different rewards? The collection comes with a simple **loot editor** — no commands, no JSON. Open a plain-text settings file, change how common each item is, how many drop, and how full each container rolls, then double-click **Build-Loot** to apply it. You can add or remove items too, and misspelled item names are caught before anything is saved. Grab it from the [GitHub collection](https://github.com/darkstarworks/minecraft-refresh-collection) and read **HOW TO CHANGE LOOT.txt**.

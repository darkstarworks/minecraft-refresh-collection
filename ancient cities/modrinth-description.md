### New **Ancient Cities** to get lost in
Bigger, more varied, and far more rewarding to explore than the vanilla deep-dark layout. This data pack overhauls Ancient City generation so every city comes out larger, with more custom buildings, bridges and structures instead of the same handful of vanilla rooms — all while the Warden still lurks in the dark below.

<br>

> **Pairs perfectly with Better Ancient Cities:** these cities still generate as regular `minecraft:ancient_city` structures, so a management plugin like Better Ancient Cities (AncientCityPro) can discover, snapshot, protect, auto-reset and manage loot. <br>
Turn your Ancient Cities into renewable, multiplayer-friendly content.

Find it alongside my other plugins in the [ESMP collection](https://modrinth.com/organization/esmp).

<br>

## Customise the loot

Want different rewards? This and the other data packs in this collection comes with a simple **loot editor**. <br>
No commands, no JSON. Just open a plain-text settings file, change how common each item is, how many drop, and how full each container rolls, then double-click **Build-Loot** to apply it. 

You can add or remove items too, and misspelled item names are caught before anything is saved. Grab it from the [GitHub collection](https://github.com/darkstarworks/minecraft-refresh-collection) and read **HOW TO CHANGE LOOT.txt**.
